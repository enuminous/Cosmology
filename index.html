01

Three different boundaries

The Hubble radius RH = c / |H| marks a recession-speed scale. Photons can cross it. It is not generally a causal horizon.

Past light reach is a(t)c∫tᵢtdt′/a(t′). This app starts at aᵢ = 10⁻⁸ (or tᵢ = −5τ in the bounce). It does not supply the omitted earlier history or model photon opacity.

The event horizon is a(t)c∫t∞dt′/a(t′), if that integral converges. It is calculated only for the Λ > 0 comparison. C1Q has no supplied future.

02

What the models mean

C1Q uses all 1,001 frozen background samples from EFMW-COSMO-C1Q v1.0.0: GR, a canonical minimally coupled scalar with V = ½m²φ², photons, baryons, CDM and three thermal neutrinos. H₀ = 67.4; m/H₀ = 1; Λ = 0. The background is homogeneous and isotropic. No empirical fit is implied.

ΛCDM comparison uses E² = Ωᵣa⁻⁴ + Ωₘa⁻³ + ΩΛ, Ωᵣ = 0.000092, ΩΛ = 1 − Ωₘ − Ωᵣ. It is a simplified fluid baseline, not the frozen package's exact massive-neutrino comparator.

Bounce prescribes a(t) = √[1 + (t/τ)²] and H = t/(t² + τ²). It is not a C1Q solution: a flat-GR bounce requires matter beyond its canonical content. The eternal toy has no finite past or future horizon. Its Hubble radius diverges at t = 0.

Recursion is a visual experiment.

The nesting depth, scale ratio, particle field, glow and camera motion are procedural artwork. They do not evolve daughter universes, global rotation, structure formation or a black-hole interior. The spatial view compresses radial distances and uses synthetic tracers; the spacetime view plots computed conformal coordinates. Neither is a telescope image. EFMW is treated as a hypothesis, not experimentally validated physics.

Calculation and precision

For N = ln a, elapsed time uses H₀⁻¹∫dN/E and conformal distance uses (c/H₀)∫e⁻ᴺdN/E. C1Q interpolates ln E between frozen nodes and integrates each segment analytically. The editable baseline uses 4,001 nodes through a = 10⁴ plus its asymptotic de Sitter tail. Readouts are approximate visualization outputs, not inference-grade likelihoods.

The light experiment integrates a radial null ray, χ(t) = χem − [η(t) − ηem], with η in comoving Gly. “Beyond available history” means exactly that; it is not proof of permanent causal disconnection.

Controls & privacy

Drag to orbit; scroll to zoom. Space plays cosmic time; ← / → move in time; R recenters; F opens focus mode; ? opens this guide. In Recursion, scrolling flies through the nested scene.

Save/open a JSON session; capture a PNG of the viewport; export the background curves as CSV. Everything runs locally. There are no fonts, libraries, trackers or datasets loaded from the network. Source links open online only when clicked. Reduced-motion preferences start the app in still mode.
