# Numerical implementation revision before any family fit

The first completed surrogate failed the unchanged numerical acceptance criteria: maximum distance/H error about 1.18e-5 and maximum |Δχ²|=0.3602. No family optimization, posterior sampling or mock calibration was run with it. Its coefficients and complete validation report are retained in `surrogate_rejected_v1/`.

Two numerical assumptions are corrected before fitting:

1. Factor the exact H0 scaling out before distance interpolation: interpolate log(h D_M), log(H/h), and 5 log10(h D_M); restore the scaling analytically in predictions and the SN magnitude offset. Represent the remaining h dependence in h^-2, its natural radiation/neutrino-density coordinate. Solve the five-node Chebyshev interpolation system at the existing transformed nodes instead of treating them as uniformly transformed Chebyshev nodes. The existing direct background grid is transformed algebraically; the physical solutions are unchanged.
2. The two-dimensional drag-ruler approximation and the assumption that reionization can be fixed both fail the declared precision target. At the middle-of-prior test point, varying z_reio from 5 to 12 changes CLASS r_drag by about 4.7e-5 relatively. Retain z_reio as a common sampled coordinate with its original uniform [5,12] prior. No new physical freedom is introduced: it was already in the original six/seven-parameter protocol. Only primordial amplitude and tilt integrate out of the distance likelihood.

Use the tighter thermodynamics numerical settings tol_thermo_integration=1e-8 and thermo_integration_stepsize=0.05 for family calculations. Retain the dense 12×14 baryon/CDM drag-ruler table, and add direct-CLASS correction tensors for its full h, baryon/CDM, scalar and reionization dependence. The correction grids are LCDM [4,3,5,5] and C1Q [4,3,5,7,5], with coordinates h, omega_b, omega_cdm, [mu], z_reio, over the original prior ranges. They require 2,400 additional direct thermodynamic calculations.

The final fitted coordinate order is [h, omega_b, omega_cdm, z_reio, log10(mu)] for C1Q and its first four coordinates for LCDM. Both retain the original independent amplitude/tilt priors, which integrate to a common constant. C1Q still adds exactly one cosmological coordinate. The revised equal optimizer budget is 60 initial points, 80 differential-evolution generations, three local starts, at most 2,000 function calls per local start and 12,000 total calls per model, identically on the data and every mock. Numerical settings are frozen by `implementation_lock.json` before the first family fit.

Validate on a fresh preselected five-dimensional Latin hypercube (50 points, seed 20261101) plus all 32 prior corners, comparing both model families directly with CLASS. The unchanged criteria are relative distance/H/r_drag errors <2e-6 and |Δχ²|<0.01 on every point. The previous validation remains visible. Any further numerical failure must be retained and resolved before inference.

This revision supersedes the approximation concerning reionization in step 2 and its associated invariance check in EXECUTION_PLAN.md. It does not change any original physical prior, action, potential, initial condition, data file, covariance, statistic, mock seed, mock count or empirical screening threshold. These are implementation corrections after observing the immutable-point likelihood, not an independently registered or blinded analysis.
